Letter press.

A letterpress prints by stamping arranged type onto paper, one impression at a time. This interface borrows that gesture to make visible the work each layer of a transformer does as it shapes a token.

The concept i.

What is the residual stream?

Inside GPT-2, every token carries a long vector of numbers that gets revised as it passes through twelve transformer layers. Each layer reads the current vector, decides what to add, and writes a small update back. That running record is the residual stream — the trace of meaning under construction.

We measure how much each layer rewrites the stream (‖Δ‖ — the magnitude of its update) and use that single number to drive how heavily that layer prints.

The interaction ii.

What you will do

  1. 01 Compose. Drag word-tokens from the case into the composing stick. Up to six.
  2. 02 Pull. Pull the lever. The form locks. The press fires once for every layer.
  3. 03 Read. Twelve sheets stack on the bed. Flip from the top down. Each impression's weight reflects how much that layer changed the residual stream.
